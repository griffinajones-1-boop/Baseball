Privacy

Short version: what you log stays on your phone.

Your game data

Games, pitches, player names and reports are saved in your browser's storage on this device. They are not uploaded to us or anyone else. There are no accounts and no ads.

  • You can export or back up your data any time from Settings.
  • You can erase everything from Settings. Clearing your browser data or removing the app also erases it.
  • Because nothing is uploaded, we can't recover lost data. Keep a backup.

Anonymous usage counts

To know whether the app is useful, it sends a few counts to our server, such as "the app was opened today", "a game was started" or "a report was shared". These contain only the name of the event: no game data, player names, device identifiers or cookies. We keep only daily totals. You can turn this off in Settings under "Share anonymous usage counts".

Feedback

If you send feedback from the app, we receive your message, your email if you choose to add one, and basic technical details (app version, mode and type of phone). Feedback is stored by our host, Netlify, and used only to answer you and improve the app.

Purchases

If you buy Pro, payment is handled by Stripe. Stripe collects your email and payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive the email used at checkout and the status of the purchase, and use them only to unlock Pro and handle billing questions. None of your game data is sent with a purchase.

Children

The app is meant for parents and guardians. It does not collect information from children; any name you type for a player stays on your device.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here. Questions: use Send feedback in the app's Settings.
